# answer, at Every Little Room

The cohort format `answer` (first approved at RoomRacer, everfields/roomracer#40), in Every Little Room's own look and with its own questions and numbers. **Not approved here yet:** `format.json` has `"approved": null` until Carlos answers this company's strategy gate.

- **Audience:** US adults buying a Gabby's Dollhouse gift: parents, and above all the grandparents, aunts and uncles they text. Never children. Every video opens by naming the adult it speaks to ("Grandparents", "Parents", "Buying a gift").
- **Promise:** one question a buyer asks before paying, answered in 15–25 seconds with the numbers our live page shows that day.
- **Hook (first second):** the buyer's question in large type, read by the voice: which dollhouse, what at my budget, what do they already have.
- **Payoff:** the answer, then the page of ours that has the rest (a guide, or the house checklist and its family link). Never a store link, never Amazon.
- **Why follow:** each Short answers the next question of the season (budget, Christmas, birthdays, stocking stuffers, new lines from the show), from a guide that says what it could not confirm.
- **Look:** the site's night palette (the house's night sky `#1F1A47`, cream `#FBF4E6`, lamp glow `#FFC964`, the pin bands' rose, mint and lilac), Inter, our wordmark. `brand.json`.
- **Voice:** Kokoro `af_heart`, a stock synthetic voice; the fine print says so. Warm, plain, one parent to another.
- **Sound:** the voice and the engine's quiet bed. No music from the show.
- **Numbers:** prices are the typical US store prices the live page shows, labelled "about", from our dated checks. Never an Amazon price. Sizes and ages only as the page states them, with "listed" where the page says listed.
- **Never appears:** the show's characters, logos, the cat-shaped house, DreamWorks, Netflix or Spin Master imagery, product photos, anything addressed to a child, games, faces, names, family story, fake urgency or counts.
- **YouTube:** uploaded with "not made for kids" (the audience is the adult buyer) and the synthetic-media flag where it applies. The title is the buyer's question. The description links to our page.
